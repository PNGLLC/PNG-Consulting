Hacked? The Owner's Recovery Checklist

Your computer was broken into, or your card details were stolen. Work down this list in order. It takes you from the first hour to the point where you can trust your systems again.

Before anything else

Do every step below from a phone or a computer you know is clean, not the one that was hacked. Anything you type on the affected machine may still be visible to whoever got in.

Where these steps come from

This checklist follows the Federal Trade Commission's guidance for people and businesses whose information has been exposed. Use the official sites for the detail on each step:

  • IdentityTheft.gov: report identity theft and get a recovery plan
  • FTC Data Breach Response: A Guide for Business
  • FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center
  • AnnualCreditReport.com

General information, not legal, financial or technical advice. Your bank, insurer and attorney know your situation and should guide the specifics.
